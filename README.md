This project is a proposed website that is designed for CHOC- Children Cancer Foundation South Africa. CHOC supports children with cancer as well as the parents of the children.it also gives teaches early warning cancer signs.
The target Audience: children with cancer ,the parents of children ,volunteers who are will to help and donors or sponsors.
The goal is :for the children to learn and know that they are not alone as well as let the parents know that they are not alone .
These colour scheme presents hope and support ,to show that we are with them at each step of the way.TO also raise awareness for children here in south africa.
A friendly user website was created to help people be able to use it.it will help provide information online.
This submission includes the planning, the structure as well as the colour pallete that will be used in the final product.
This website only consists of html only.
